Questions & answers

FAQ

Straight answers — and where something depends on your exact rental, we say so instead of guessing. Anything else: WhatsApp us, pre-rental questions get answered fast.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

No. Sun Drive Cars rents without a deposit — nothing is blocked on your account before, during or after the rental. It's the core of how we work and the most-mentioned topic in our Google reviews (16 mentions).

Do I need a credit card?

No. You can book and collect a car without a credit card. As one guest wrote: “without deposit and credit card!”

How much does a rental cost?

We don't publish a generic price list. Send your dates and pickup point on WhatsApp and you'll get a personal quote in minutes, confirmed in writing before you commit. Guests describe our pricing as honest, with no hidden fees.

How does airport pickup work?

Reserve on WhatsApp, send your flight number, and we meet you at Agadir Al Massira arrivals — the car waits in the airport carpark. Handover takes minutes.

My flight lands late at night — is that a problem?

No. We operate 24/7, and late-night handovers are routine: one reviewer's car “was there on time at the airport even though it was nearly 11pm”.

How do I return the car at the airport?

Park in the airport carpark, leave the keys in the car, and head to departures. Guests consistently describe returns as a two-minute process.

Can you deliver the car to my hotel?

Yes — we deliver to and collect from hotels in Agadir, day and night. Guests have also left the keys at their hotel reception for us to collect.

What about insurance, fuel policy and driver requirements?

These are confirmed with your quote on WhatsApp, in writing, before you book. We'd rather confirm the exact conditions for your dates than publish fine print that might not match your rental.

How do I pay?

No credit card is required and nothing is prepaid online. The payment method for your rental is confirmed with your quote on WhatsApp.

Which cars do you have?

Recent Dacia models — the Logan sedan and the Sandero hatchback, both current-generation, 5 seats. Guests have also toured in a Dacia Duster; ask us what's available for your dates.

What happens if the car has a problem on the road?

You message us and we sort it. When one guest broke down, a replacement car was brought out within a couple of hours — their account, in our reviews. That's how we handle problems: personally and fast.

Where is your office?

10 Rue Ifni, in Talborjt — Agadir's historic central district, a short walk from Boulevard Mohammed V. Plus code CCF3+4C. But you don't have to come to us: we deliver at the airport and at hotels 24/7.
